## Strategy Context

This account runs a **"Hammer" content consumption strategy**:
- Engagement/awareness campaigns drive content consumption at the top of funnel
- Direct in-platform conversions are **not** the success metric
- Real conversions happen downstream: prospects → application calls → sales calls → $7K deals
- Application calls and closed deals are tracked outside of Meta's pixel attribution

This means **Meta-attributed conversion numbers significantly understate true performance**.

## What to Watch / Improve

### 1. Frequency Approaching Fatigue Zone
- Hammer Them - Carousels: **15.45x frequency** (30 days)
- Hammer Them - Social Proof Videos: **11.96x frequency**
- Hammer Them - Short Form: **9.69x frequency**

These are still part of the hammer strategy, but rising CPCs (last 3 days vs last 30) suggest fatigue is starting. **Plan creative refresh in the next 2-3 weeks** before efficiency drops more.

### 2. Tracking Gap = Attribution Blindness
You can't see in Meta which engagement campaigns are actually feeding the calls that close. Consider:
- Adding a UTM-tagged source field to the application form
- Asking on the application form: "Which video brought you here?"
- This would let you measure which "hammer" content actually drives the booked calls

### 3. Reactivating Retargeting Could Compound Returns
The MOF - IG Retargeting Social Proof campaign was paused, but logically it should catch the people who consumed the hammer content. With 1M+ reach on engagement campaigns in 30 days, there's a massive warm audience ready to be retargeted toward the application. **Consider reactivating with fresh creative aimed at booking calls.**

### 4. Venus Flytrap Educational Has No Tracking
42K link clicks/month is sending people somewhere — but with no pixel/UTM tracking, you can't see what they do after the click. Even a simple landing page conversion event would tell you which of these visitors become applications.

### 5. Hammer Them - Social Proof Videos Underperforming
$1,212 spent / 1 link click / 11.96x frequency. Even by content consumption standards, this one is showing fatigue. Consider refreshing the creative or reducing budget allocation toward Short Form which is performing better at similar CPM.
