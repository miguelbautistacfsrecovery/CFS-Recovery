# 7-Day Free Trial: Onboarding SMS Drip

**Audience:** People who just started the Recovery Academy 7-day free trial.
**Goal:** Keep them engaged, drive the core trial actions, and handle the Day 8 auto-continue transparently.
**Sender:** Send from a real person (Dana or Brent, our Client Coordinators) so replies reach a human.
**Voice:** Super casual, like texting a friend. No em dashes. No guarantees, cures, or recovery timelines.

**Cadence:** One text per day, Day 0 to Day 8 (roughly one per day, very light).
**Placeholders:** swap `[ ]` for your real Skool deep links. `{first_name}` = member first name.

---

## The sequence

**Day 0 (instantly on signup)**
> hey {first_name}, you're in 🙌 it's Dana from CFS Recovery. i'll check in a little this week so your 7 days actually count, nothing spammy promise. today just watch Day 1's short videos, that's the whole job 👉 [modules]
> (txt STOP anytime to stop these)

**Day 1**
> morning {first_name}! how'd Day 1 land? today's modules just unlocked and they build on each other so the order matters 👉 [modules] even 5 mins counts 🙂

**Day 2 (the assessment)**
> quick one {first_name} 👀 remember that nervous system assessment we promised? few mins and you get a personalized snapshot of where you're starting 👉 [assessment] kinda cool to see

**Day 3 (live call)**
> the live group calls are honestly the best part {first_name}. next one's coming up, come hang even if you just listen 👉 [calendar] no need to talk or turn your cam on, promise

**Day 4 (social proof, for a hard day)**
> if today's a heavier one {first_name}, go read a few Wins from other members 👉 [wins] real people who started right where you are. borrow their proof for a min 💛

**Day 5 (participate)**
> tiny challenge {first_name} 😄 post ONE question in the community today, anything you're wondering about 👉 [questions] fastest way to actually feel part of it

**Day 6 (the honest heads-up)**
> heads up {first_name}, your trial wraps tomorrow ⏳ if you're feeling it you don't have to do a thing, you'll roll into full membership on Day 8. if it's not for you, totally cool, cancel here anytime 👉 [cancel] no awkwardness

**Day 7 (last day + what staying unlocks)**
> last day {first_name}! this week was just the first 7 of a full 30-day path that keeps unlocking. stay on and you'll book a roadmap call + build your own 30-day plan with a coach. hope you stick around 🙌

**Day 8 (welcome to membership)**
> you're officially in {first_name} 🎉 so glad you stayed. next step is getting your roadmap call booked so we can build your plan 👉 [book call] me or Brent are right here if you need anything

## Optional layers (recommended once the base sequence is live)

- **Event-based call reminders:** a "starts in 2 hrs, here's the link 👉 [join]" text a few hours before each Monday and Thursday call. Usually the highest-engagement text you can send.
- **Branching logic:**
  - Took the assessment already → skip the Day 2 reminder.
  - Cancelled → stop the drip immediately.
  - No module opened by Day 3 → softer nudge: "stuck on where to start {first_name}? reply here and I'll point you to the right first video."
- **Completion celebration:** if they finish all 7 days of modules, a quick "you did the whole first week, that consistency is exactly the point, proud of you 🙌"

## Compliance / deliverability notes

- Day 0 identifies the sender and includes a STOP opt-out (both done above). Keep STOP honored automatically.
- One link per text. Send during daytime in the member's own timezone.
- No recovery guarantees or timelines in any message, matching the workshop deck's voice rules.
