# Post-Event SMS Blasts (Broadcast)

**Audience:** Event registrants and attendees who have NOT started the free trial yet.
(Different from the onboarding drip, which goes to people who already signed up.)
**Window:** The 3 days after the "5 Critical Shifts" virtual event.
**Goal:** Drive 7-day free trial signups before the 150 spots / timer close.
**Sender:** Dana or Brent, for a human feel.
**Voice:** Casual, warm. No em dashes. No guarantees or recovery timelines. Scarcity truthful (150 spots, timer).
**Placeholders:** swap `[link]` for the real trial URL. `{first_name}` = recipient first name.

---

## The blasts

**Blast 1 (Day 1, morning after the event)**
> hey {first_name}, it's Dana from CFS Recovery 🙌 the 5 Shifts workshop replay is up for a short time, and you can try the whole Recovery Academy free for 7 days (only 150 spots). watch + grab your spot 👉 [link]
> (txt STOP to opt out)

**Blast 2 (Day 2)**
> quick one {first_name} 👀 your free 7 days gets you the daily modules, live coaching calls, the community, and your nervous system assessment. normally $297/mo, free to try, no contract. spots are filling 👉 [link]

**Blast 3 (Day 3, morning)**
> {first_name} today's the last day for the free trial spots ⏳ once the 150 are gone it's back to regular price. if you've been thinking about it, now's the moment 👉 [link]

**Blast 4 (Day 3, evening / final hours)**
> last call {first_name} 🚪 trial spots close tonight. it's free for 7 days, cancel anytime, literally no risk to look around. don't let this one slip by 👉 [link]

---

## Optional add
- **Day 1 evening (only if your list tolerates 2/day):** a short "in case you missed it this morning, the replay + free trial are live, spots are limited 👉 [link]" resend to non-clickers only.

## Compliance notes
- Blast 1 identifies the sender and includes STOP. Suppress anyone who already started the trial (they get the onboarding drip instead) and anyone who has opted out.
- One link per message. Send during daytime hours per recipient timezone.
- Cap at one blast per day except the Day 3 morning + final-hours pair, so the broadcast list does not feel spammed.
